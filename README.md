# 🇮🇳 Nirvaran Setu

### AI-Powered Citizen Grievance Management Platform

Nirvaran Setu is an AI-assisted civic grievance platform designed to make it easier for citizens to report, track, and verify the resolution of civic issues.

The platform allows citizens to submit grievances using text and optional multimedia evidence. AI analyses the grievance, identifies the issue category and responsible department, and proposes a suitable solution. Administrators can then manage the grievance workflow until the citizen verifies the resolution.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Citizens often face difficulties when reporting civic problems such as potholes, drainage issues, water supply problems, waste management, streetlight failures, and other local infrastructure issues.

Common challenges include:

- Difficulty identifying the correct department
- Lack of transparency after submitting a complaint
- Limited ability to provide evidence
- Manual grievance classification and routing
- Lack of citizen involvement in verifying resolution
- Poor visibility into the overall grievance-management process

Nirvaran Setu addresses these challenges through an AI-assisted, transparent grievance workflow.

---

## 💡 Proposed Solution

Nirvaran Setu provides a single platform where citizens can:

1. Describe a civic issue.
2. Provide its location.
3. Upload optional photo, video, or audio evidence.
4. Submit the grievance for AI analysis.
5. Automatically identify the issue category.
6. Automatically route the grievance to the appropriate department.
7. Track the grievance through every stage.
8. Verify whether the issue has actually been resolved.

Administrators receive a dedicated dashboard to monitor grievances and move them through the operational workflow.

### 🤖 AI-Assisted Analysis

The AI analysis system processes the submitted grievance and helps determine:

- Issue category
- Responsible department
- Grievance summary
- AI confidence
- Proposed solution

The AI is responsible for classification and routing, while the administrative side manages the operational workflow.
